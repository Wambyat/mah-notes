#### Ingredients:
1. ಅವಲಕ್ಕಿ (flattened rice): Medium thickness [^1]
2. Neutral Oil like vegetable/sunflower/ricebran or peanut oil - for [[ವಗ್ಗರಣೆ - Vagaranne]]
3. ಕಡಲೆಕಾಯಿ (Peanuts), ಗೋಡಂಬಿ (Cashew)
4. ಕಡಲೆ ಬೇಳೆ (Chana Dal), ಉದ್ದಿನ ಬೇಳೆ (Uradd Dal), ಸಾಸಿವೆ (Mustard seed), ಜೀರಿಗೆ (Jeera seeds), (Optional) ಇಂಗು (Asafoetida)
5. ಕರಿಬೇವು (Curry leaves), Vegetables like ಗಜರಿ (Carrot), ಆಲೂಗಡ್ಡೆ (Potato), ದೊಡ್ಡ ಮೆಣಸಿನಕಾಯಿ (Capsicum), ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ (Onion), ಪಾಲಕ್ (Spinach), ಶುಂಠಿ (Ginger).
6. ಅರಿಶಿನ (Turmeric powder), ಉಪ್ಪು (Salt) to cook
7. ತುರಿದ ತೆಂಗಿನಕಾಯಿ (shredded dry coconut flesh), ಕೊತ್ತಂಬರಿ (Coriander leaves)
#### Steps:
1. Wash Ingredient 1.
2. Add Ingredient 2 to a large pot/pan and heat till hot. 
3. Add Ingredient 3 and fry till desired. Remove and set aside.
4. Add all Ingredient 4 wait till cooked as per liking.
5. Add all Ingredient 5, top with Ingredient 6 cook till desired.
6. Add Ingredient 1 and mix. IF NEEDED add little water to help mix.
7. Top with Ingredient 5 and ಉಪ್ಪು (Salt) to taste.
###### Variants:
- If ಟೊಮೆಟೊ (Tomato) is needed add at end of Step 4 and cook for a bit.
- Can add Mint, Garam Masala to make like Pulav. 
- White ಅವಲಕ್ಕಿ: Low caramelised ಈರುಳ್ಳಿ, ಅವಲಕ್ಕಿ and Ingredient 7 and ಶುಂಠಿ.
- Remove ತುರಿದ ತೆಂಗಿನಕಾಯಿ if you need to store for a longer time. Upto 2 days in fridge, upto 15 days in freezer.

[^1]: Use medium thickness as this just needs to be washed and used immediately. Thin cannot be used here. If you use the fat one, it needs to be soaked for one hour.
